# A Day of One-Line Pitches

[![make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ronigrv-a-day-of-one-line-pitches-12cd9bb3)](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ronigrv-a-day-of-one-line-pitches-12cd9bb3)

## The pitch

> Give your app idea to an AI agent on make.fun and watch it become a real app before your coffee gets cold - no coding, no jargon, just tell it what you want and it builds it.

One sentence, 174 characters, for a friend who has never coded. The hook is
the coffee going cold: the app exists before a small, everyday wait is over.
"No coding, no jargon" takes away the two fears a beginner has — that they
need to learn to code, and that they won't understand the words — and "just
tell it what you want and it builds it" leaves only the easy part: describing
the idea out loud, the way you would to a friend.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt` — one sentence, under 240
characters, valid UTF-8 — and prints it.
